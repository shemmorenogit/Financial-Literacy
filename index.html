Why Kenyan Students Crash in Week 3

Spot the Trick

Kenyan scammers target teens with fake prizes and rogue loan apps.

Spot the Trick Scam Radar
Incoming SMS / WhatsApp

"CONGRATULATIONS! You won KSh 50,000 from the Promo Draw.
Send KSh 500 facilitation fee to claim it."

Red Flags show here:

Legitimate Safaricom or bank promotions NEVER ask you to send money first to receive a prize!

Wireframe Screen 7

Money at Home

Decoding the financial tools Kenyan parents and adults use every day.

Money at Home Knowledge Vault
Fuliza High Daily Fees

• What it is: Safaricom's automatic overdraft service when you don't have enough M-Pesa balance at the till or paybill.

• How it works: Any money sent to your phone later is instantly deducted to clear the debt.

• One risk: Facility charges compound daily; defaulting ruins credit scores once you register with an ID at 18.

🗣️ Ask your parent: "What does Fuliza actually cost our family each month?"
